Orientační posouzení pro IT provoz

AI Agent Readiness Advisor

Průvodce do ChatGPT, Clauda, Gemini nebo Copilota. Projde s vámi místa v IT provozu, kam chcete pustit AI, a u každého řekne, jestli stačí asistent, nebo dává smysl agent, kolik autonomie mu dát a co musí být měřené a vidět, než ji dostane.

Připravili jsme ho pro Grafana AI Observability Breakfast, 25. 11. 2026, Praha.

Nastavení a první test: zhruba 20 minut
Nastavit v chatu

Proč tenhle nástroj

AI se v provozu zkouší na třídění alertů, shrnutí incidentů, návrzích oprav i na zásazích, které agent udělá sám. U každého takového místa padá stejná otázka: co smí agent udělat bez člověka, kdo ho schvaluje a podle čeho poznáte, že to dělá dobře.

Průvodce tu otázku projde s vámi, místo po místu. Popíšete vlastními slovy, co by AI měla dělat. Průvodce se doptá a ke každému místu řekne, kterou úroveň autonomie chcete a kterou váš popis dnes unese. Rozdíl mezi nimi ukazuje, co je potřeba doplnit a změřit.

Výchozí postoj je least agency: dát agentovi jen tolik samostatnosti, kolik úloha potřebuje. OWASP k tomu v Top 10 for Agentic Applications for 2026 píše, že bez jasného přehledu o tom, co agenti dělají, proč a jaké nástroje volají, může zbytečná autonomie nenápadně zvětšovat plochu útoku.

Co dělá a nedělá

Průvodce mluví řečí provozu. Pojem z agentního světa (human in the loop, guardrail, evaluace) vysvětlí jednou větou, když ho poprvé použije.

Doptá se

Nejvýš tři otázky v kole. Když něco nevíte, odpovíte „nevím“ a průvodce to vezme jako předpoklad.

Zařadí každé místo

Do jedné ze čtyř úrovní autonomie podle nejrizikovější akce, kterou agent technicky může udělat bez člověka. Rozhodují práva a nástroje. Instrukce v promptu úroveň nesnižují.

Projde brány se semaforem

Sedm podmínek od dohledatelnosti kroků agenta po vypínač. U každé 🟢, 🟡 nebo 🔴 s důvodem.

Řekne, co měřit

Co musí být vidět, než agent dostane víc práv, a kde to v Grafaně uvidíte, včetně stavu funkce (GA, public preview).

Kroky a decision record

Nejvýš pět kroků, které jde udělat hned. Poslední je vždy decision record pro každé místo a průvodce ho nabídne předvyplněný.

Co průvodce nedělá

Nikdy neřekne, že je agent připravený nebo bezpečný. Nejsilnější formulace, kterou použije, je „pro tuhle úroveň podle popisu nevidím zjevnou mezeru“. Instrumentaci, dashboardy, dotazy (PromQL, LogQL, TraceQL) ani konfiguraci Alloy neřeší, na to má Grafana oficiální sadu agent skills grafana/skills. Nevybírá AI produkt ani dodavatele, nepočítá návratnost a neposuzuje právní stránku. Na AI Act odkáže na AI Act Advisor.

Úrovně autonomie a brány

Úrovně jsou pracovní stupnice průvodce. Každé místo dostane dvě: tu, kterou chcete, a tu, kterou váš popis dnes unese.

Asistent radí

Čte data a navrhuje, akci udělá člověk. Třeba shrnutí incidentu do kanálu.

Agent jedná se schválením

Akci provede, až ji člověk schválí. Třeba restart služby po kliknutí operátora.

Agent jedná sám v mezích

Jedná bez schválení, jen v uzavřeném seznamu akcí a v mezích, které vynucuje něco mimo model: práva v cílovém systému, seznam povolených nástrojů. Spouští ho člověk nebo událost, třeba alert.

Agent jedná sám

Spouští se sám, běží průběžně a sám určuje, kdy a co udělá.

Mapování na úrovně autonomie podle Feng a kol. a na AWS Agentic AI Security Scoping Matrix je náš výklad a průvodce ho tak označuje.

Brány

Brána je podmínka, která musí být splněná a změřená, než agent na daném místě dostane danou úroveň. Pro vyšší úroveň je víc bran blokujících.

  • Dohledatelnost
  • Kvalita a evaluace
  • Náklady na úlohu
  • Rollback a vypínač
  • Schvalování
  • Auditní stopa
  • Co nejmenší autonomie

Semafor

  • 🟢podle popisu nevidím zjevnou mezeru
  • 🟡chybí informace nebo měření, doplňte
  • 🔴tuhle úroveň autonomie bych zatím nedával

Semafor hodnotí jen popis, který dáte. Odpověď „nevím“ je 🟡. Průvodce dá 🔴 vždy, když má agent jednat sám a chybí vypínač nebo jméno toho, kdo ho smí použít, když agent zapisuje do produkce a není vidět, co udělal, když je dopad chyby nevratný a chybí schválení a když meze hlídá jen prompt. K 🔴 u kvality přidá cestu ven: měsíc nechat agenta jednat jen se schválením a měřit, kolik návrhů člověk zamítne.

Decision record

Decision record je jednostránkový záznam o tom, že agent smí na konkrétním místě jednat na konkrétní úrovni autonomie. Je v něm, kdo to rozhodl, s jakými nástroji a právy agent pracuje, co se měří a do kdy povolení platí. Když se za půl roku někdo zeptá, proč agent v noci restartoval službu, odpovědí je tenhle záznam se jménem, datem a důvodem.

Zakládejte ho pro každé místo, kde agent jedná se schválením nebo sám, při každém zvýšení úrovně a pokaždé, když agent dostane nový nástroj nebo nová práva.

Indicative assessment for IT operations

AI Agent Readiness Advisor

A guide for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot. It goes through the places in your IT operations where you want to use AI and, for each one, tells you whether an assistant is enough or an agent makes sense, how much autonomy to give it and what must be measured and visible before it gets that autonomy.

Prepared for the Grafana AI Observability Breakfast, 25 November 2026, Prague.

Setup and first test: about 20 minutes
Set up in chat

Why this tool

Teams are trying AI in operations for alert triage, incident summaries, fix proposals and actions an agent takes on its own. Every one of these places raises the same question: what may the agent do without a person, who approves it and how do you know it is doing a good job.

The guide works through that question with you, one place at a time. You describe in your own words what the AI should do. The guide asks follow-up questions and, for each place, tells you the level of autonomy you want and the level your description supports today. The gap between the two shows what you still need to add and measure.

The default stance is least agency: give the agent only as much autonomy as the task needs. OWASP's Top 10 for Agentic Applications for 2026 notes that without clear visibility into what agents are doing, why, and which tools they invoke, unnecessary autonomy can quietly expand the attack surface.

What it does and does not do

The guide speaks the language of operations. The first time it uses an agent term (human in the loop, guardrail, evaluation), it explains it in one sentence.

Asks questions

At most three per round. If you don't know something, say so and the guide treats it as an assumption.

Classifies each place

Into one of four autonomy levels, based on the riskiest action the agent can technically take without a person. Permissions and tools decide. Instructions in the prompt do not lower the level.

Checks gates with a traffic light

Seven conditions, from traceability of the agent's steps to the kill switch. Each gets 🟢, 🟡 or 🔴 and a reason.

Tells you what to measure

What must be visible before the agent gets more permissions, and where you will see it in Grafana, including the feature status (GA, public preview).

Steps and a decision record

At most five steps you can take now. The last one is always a decision record for each place, and the guide offers to pre-fill it.

What the guide does not do

It never says that an agent is ready or safe. The strongest wording it uses is "for this level I see no obvious gap from the description". It does not cover instrumentation, dashboards, queries (PromQL, LogQL, TraceQL) or Alloy configuration; Grafana's official agent skills set grafana/skills covers that. It does not pick an AI product or vendor, does not calculate return and does not assess the legal side. For the AI Act it points you to the AI Act Advisor.

Autonomy levels and gates

The levels are the guide's working scale. Each place gets two: the level you want and the level your description supports today.

Assistant advises

Reads data and proposes, a person takes the action. For example an incident summary posted to a channel.

Agent acts with approval

Takes the action after a person approves it. For example a service restart after the operator clicks to confirm.

Agent acts on its own within limits

Acts without approval, but only from a closed list of actions and within limits enforced outside the model: permissions in the target system, an allowlist of tools. A person or an event, such as an alert, triggers it.

Agent acts on its own

Triggers itself, runs continuously and decides by itself when and what to do.

The mapping to the autonomy levels by Feng et al. and to the AWS Agentic AI Security Scoping Matrix is our interpretation, and the guide labels it that way.

Gates

A gate is a condition that has to be met and measured before the agent gets a given level at a given place. The higher the level, the more gates are blocking.

  • Traceability
  • Quality and evaluation
  • Cost per task
  • Rollback and kill switch
  • Approval
  • Audit trail
  • Least agency

Traffic light

  • 🟢from the description I see no obvious gap
  • 🟡information or measurement is missing, please add it
  • 🔴I would not give this level of autonomy yet

The traffic light rates only the description you give. "I don't know" is 🟡. The guide always gives 🔴 when the agent is to act alone and there is no kill switch or no name of who may use it, when the agent writes to production and what it did is not visible, when the impact of an error is irreversible and there is no approval, and when only the prompt enforces the limits. With 🔴 on quality it adds a way out: a month in which the agent acts only with approval, measuring how many proposals a person rejects.

What to measure and where to see it in Grafana

For each measurement it recommends, the guide tells you where Grafana shows it, with the feature name and its status as of the source verification date. It does not describe the setup; that is in grafana/skills. If you don't use Grafana Cloud, it points to the open OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions, which are in Development status.

Benchmark case study

Decision record

A decision record is a one-page note stating that an agent may act at a given autonomy level at a given place. It records who decided, which tools and permissions the agent works with, what is measured and until when the permission is valid. When someone asks six months later why the agent restarted a service at night, this record with a name, a date and a reason is the answer.

Create one for every place where the agent acts with approval or on its own, every time the level goes up and every time the agent gets a new tool or new permissions.

You can download a filled-in example for an automatic service restart and a blank form. Do not upload it to the project; it is a template for you. The guide has the form in the method file and pre-fills the decision record for you. The company, names, numbers and deadlines in the example are made up. Take the form and set the values for your own operations.

decision-record-example.mddecision-record-priklad.md

Limitations

The assessment is indicative and based only on what you describe. It does not assess the model's resistance to attacks, the legal side (AI Act, data protection), product choice or whether the deployment pays off.

It is valid as of the date it was made. Go through it again when the agent gets a new tool or new permissions, when you change the model or prompt, when you want to raise the level and after every incident the agent caused.

When something doesn't work

What goes where?

The text of project-instructions goes into the instructions field. Two files go into the knowledge base: agent-readiness-complete (method) and agent-readiness-sources (sources). Both go to the same place, Sources or Knowledge depending on the platform, one after the other. The decision record example does not belong in the project.

Should I download .txt or .md?

Use .txt by default. It works on all platforms, including Microsoft 365 Copilot, which does not accept .md for direct upload. Both variants have the same content. Claude Projects accept .md as well.

Download doesn't work

Use the "Copy text" button on the card and paste the content by hand, for example into Notepad, then save it as .txt. This mostly affects some Safari versions, which have documented problems downloading generated files.

How far does a free account get me?

ChatGPT Free allows 5 files per project and 3 file uploads per day per account. The guide needs two files, so you use 2 of your 3 daily uploads. Claude Free allows 5 projects per account, with practically no limit on files. A Gemini skill has a 100 MB limit for all files together. Two files are fine on every platform.

I don't have projects, or my company has disabled them

Use the agent-readiness-all-in-one card and the "Fallback" tab. It works in a regular chat on any platform, but only for one conversation, and it is the shortened version with limited function: no question bank, no detailed gate descriptions and no source citations.

The guide gives an assessment straight away without asking anything

Check that the project-instructions text is in the project instructions field. Uploading it only as a file to the knowledge base is not enough.

The guide marks claims about Grafana as unverified

The agent-readiness-sources file is probably missing. Upload it to the knowledge base and start a new chat. Without it, the guide should mark every claim about products and standards as unverified. If you want to know where a claim comes from, ask. The guide gives the name of the source and a link.

Microsoft 365 Copilot looks different from the instructions

It depends on whether your company has a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence or pay-as-you-go and whether the administrator enabled it. Without them, the agent won't take files as knowledge. If you don't see "New agent", use option B in the Microsoft 365 Copilot tab, or the "Fallback" tab.

Is the result an audit?

No. It is an indicative assessment based on what you describe. It does not confirm that the agent is ready or safe, and the decision about its autonomy stays with you.

The button looks different from the picture

Platform interfaces change. The diagrams show the screen layout and the order of steps; the current look may differ. Look for buttons by name or function.

It doesn't work on my phone

Set up the project, skill or agent on a computer. Agent Builder in Microsoft 365 Copilot is not available on mobile, and a Gem on Google Workspace accounts can only be created on a computer. ChatGPT and Claude Projects seem to work on mobile, but the interface may differ, so check in the app. Chatting with a finished project or agent works on mobile; for a Gemini skill, check in the app.
