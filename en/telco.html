Telco Monitoring & Observability

Solution Overview

End-to-end network and service visibility – from infrastructure to the customer

Modern telco networks combine legacy OSS, multi-vendor NMS, and new cloud-native components. Without a unified view of network health and its impact on services, you respond to incidents late and without context. TSP DATA delivers an observability platform that connects technical data with the service and business layer.

Core Capabilities

What we cover – from raw network event collection and correlation to business-level service assurance and proactive capacity planning.

Fault & Performance Monitoring

Real-time monitoring of network and services with event correlation and SLA tracking. Detect anomalies before they escalate into customer-impacting outages.

Service Modeling (RFS/CFS)

Map technical resources to business services using a structured RFS/CFS model. Visualize dependencies and topology to understand exactly which services are affected by any network change.

Event Correlation & Enrichment

Reduce alert fatigue through intelligent correlation and suppression. Events are enriched with context from CMDB and external data sources, giving NOC teams the information they need to act decisively.

EMS/NMS Integration

Native integration with multi-vendor environments via APIs, purpose-built adapters, and data pipelines. Connect existing element management and network management systems without rip-and-replace.

NOC Transformation

Unified NOC dashboard consolidating all event streams into a single operational view. Automate repetitive tasks and integrate with ITSM to reduce mean time to resolution.

SLA Assurance & Reporting

Real-time SLA dashboards with proactive breach notifications. Business-level reporting enables service delivery managers and leadership to track performance against contractual commitments.

Typical Use Cases

From OSS modernization to real-time operational intelligence – scenarios where TSP DATA delivers measurable impact.

OSS Modernization

  • Migration from legacy OSS platforms (Netcool, Watch4Net, HP NNMi) to a modern unified observability stack
  • Unified monitoring across multi-vendor networks – access, transport, core, and cloud edge
  • Centralization of event management from dozens of NMS systems into a single operational view

Operational Intelligence

  • Service impact analysis – automatic determination of the customer service impact of any network fault
  • Real-time service health dashboards for management and NOC teams
  • Proactive SLA breach detection with automated escalation workflows
