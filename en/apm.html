Solution Overview

See everything. Understand why. Resolve faster.

Distributed applications fail in ways that individual monitoring tools miss. TSP DATA delivers full-stack observability – connecting metrics, logs, traces, and user experience into a single coherent picture. Whether you run microservices on Kubernetes or hybrid legacy systems, we give your teams the context to resolve incidents before they impact users.

Core Capabilities

End-to-end observability across applications, infrastructure, and user experience – from a single, coherent platform.

Distributed Tracing

End-to-end request tracing across microservices, databases, and external APIs with OpenTelemetry. Pinpoint latency and errors precisely across complex call chains, regardless of the number of services involved.

Metrics, Logs & Traces Correlation

Unified signal view that connects the what, when, and why of every incident. Switch seamlessly between metrics, logs, and traces in a single workflow to reduce investigation time from hours to minutes.

Application Dependency Mapping

Automatic discovery and visualization of service dependencies and runtime topology. Understand how components interact in production – without maintaining documentation manually.

User Experience Monitoring

Real user monitoring (RUM) and synthetic checks tied directly to business transactions. Detect degraded user journeys before they escalate into support tickets or lost revenue.

AI-Assisted Root Cause Analysis

Faster incident resolution through automated anomaly detection and causal chains. Surface the probable root cause within seconds, not after a war-room session spanning multiple teams.

Hybrid & Multi-Cloud Coverage

Consistent observability across cloud-native, containerized, and legacy environments. One operational model for Kubernetes workloads, on-prem systems, and everything in between.
